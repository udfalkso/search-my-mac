# Search My Mac privacy policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

Search My Mac is developed by Udi Falkson. It searches documents locally on your Mac.

## Documents and search

The Mac App Store edition reads folders you explicitly select. It stores a local index containing file metadata, extracted document text, matching passages, and optional semantic embeddings. Documents, document text, embeddings, and search queries are not uploaded to the developer or an AI service.

Search history and saved searches are stored locally. You can disable history recording and clear existing history in Settings. Removing an indexed folder removes that folder's search data without deleting the original documents.

## Optional semantic model

Semantic search requires an optional model download. The app verifies the download's size and SHA-256 checksum before using it. Model inference runs on your Mac. Download hosts receive ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, when delivering the model. No document content or search query is included in the download request.

## Analytics and diagnostics

The app does not include advertising, tracking, or developer-operated telemetry. It writes local diagnostic information about app operation and performance. Diagnostic files are not automatically sent to the developer. Apple may separately process App Store purchases, crash reports, or analytics according to Apple's policies and your device settings.

## Purchases and trial

The Mac App Store edition offers a seven-day free trial and a one-time full unlock. Apple processes purchases through StoreKit. The app verifies Apple-signed purchase records locally to determine whether the trial or full unlock is active. No developer-operated purchase server receives these records. Starting a trial does not authorize an automatic charge.

## Your control

You choose which folders to index, can pause indexing, and can remove indexed folders. The index and local configuration are stored in the app's local macOS storage. Original documents remain in their original locations. Local data may be included in backups you configure; Search My Mac does not operate a cloud backup service.

## Support

If you contact support, the information you choose to provide will be used to address your request. Do not post private documents, sensitive file paths, search queries, or unreviewed diagnostic logs to a public issue.

For privacy questions, [open a support issue](https://github.com/udfalkso/search-my-mac/issues). GitHub handles information submitted through its service under its own privacy policy.

## Changes

This policy will be updated when the app's privacy practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
